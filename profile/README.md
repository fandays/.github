## 🏟️ Who we are

FanDays is a Danish company for football fans.
We believe the best moments in football happen in the stadium:
the walk to the ground, the noise when the teams come out, the goal you'll never forget.
Our job is to make getting there easy.

## 🎟️ What we offer

### Fodboldpakker: football packages
Match ticket, travel and hotel in one package, with one clear price.
The big leagues, the classic derbies and the grounds you've always dreamed of.

### Fan experiences
More than 90 minutes: stadium tours, hospitality, group trips for friends and supporters' clubs,
and days built around the club you love.

### For companies and groups
Football is a great way to bring people together. We put together trips for teams,
customers and colleagues, and we take care of the details.

## 💚 Our promise

- **Everything in one place.** One booking covers the whole day.
- **Clear prices.** What you see is what you pay.
- **Made by fans.** We know what a match day should feel like, because we live for them too.
- **Here when it matters.** Before, during and after your trip.

## 🛠️ On GitHub

This is where we build the FanDays and Fodboldpakker platforms:
the website fans use to find and book trips, and the tools our team uses to make every match day run smoothly.
